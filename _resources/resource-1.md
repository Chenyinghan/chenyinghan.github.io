This album is a survival guide for incoming students of PKU Tong Class, covering onboarding information, course selection, introductory programming, recommended resources, and advice for beginning research. I served as the chief editors of the guide. Although it is written with Tong Class freshmen in mind, many parts should also be helpful for AI/CS freshmen at Peking University and other universities.
